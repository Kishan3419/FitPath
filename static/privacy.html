Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains how FitPath handles information when you use this exercise and wellness application. FitPath is operated by FitPath in India. It is a starter service, not a medical provider.

Information we handle

  • Account details: name, email address, optional age, fitness level, goal, and food preference. Passwords are stored as salted password hashes, not readable passwords.
  • Activity: exercise completion dates and exercise identifiers, used to show your history, progress, and streaks.
  • Google sign-in: if enabled and you choose it, Google shares an identity token and verified account details (such as your email, name, and stable account identifier) so FitPath can authenticate you. Google sign-in is optional.
  • AI coach messages: if AI chat is enabled, the message and recent chat context you submit are sent to Google Gemini to generate a reply. Do not include passwords, contact details, or sensitive medical information. FitPath does not save chat history in its account database, but Google may process prompts under its own terms and policies.
  • Technical data: the server and hosting provider may process network and diagnostic information necessary to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

FitPath uses account and activity information to provide sign-in, personalize the exercise library to your selected preferences, save completed activities, calculate streaks, and respond to support requests. It is not sold or used for advertising by FitPath.

Where it is stored and shared

Account and exercise-log data is stored in the PostgreSQL database configured by the service operator. Google receives data when you choose Google sign-in or send an AI coach message. Hosting, database, and email providers may process information to provide their services. Their handling is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and your choices

Account and activity data is retained while the account is active and may remain in backups for a limited period. To request access, correction, or deletion of your account data, contact the operator below. FitPath currently does not offer an in-app account deletion control. You may decline Google sign-in and AI chat and may stop using the service at any time.

Security and children

The operator uses reasonable technical safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not designed for children.
